# Mission: Algorithmic problem solving (C++)

## Why
Build real problem-solving ability, not for a specific interview or contest. The aim is to face an unfamiliar problem and reach an optimal solution without looking it up. The current weak spots are the "black box" topics (recursion/trees, graphs, DP) and getting from brute force to optimal.

## Success looks like
- Solves a LeetCode Hard in recursion/backtracking, graphs or DP unaided, and can explain why the approach is optimal
- Given a new problem, names the candidate technique(s) from its constraints and wording before coding
- Turns a working brute force into the optimal solution by spotting the redundant work

## Constraints
- 3+ month horizon, no hard deadline
- Solutions in C++, committed to `leetcode/` with a `main` that runs examples (existing repo style)
- Learner picks specific problems they want to crack, and lessons are built around those

## Out of scope
- Contest-specific tricks (fast I/O, templates for rating grind)
- Interview soft skills / system design
